❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When will the free beta end?

We plan to transition to our commission model in Q3 2026. All sellers who join during beta will receive a 3-month grace period before any commission applies.

Is there any setup fee or deposit?

Absolutely none. Joining VyaparSync is completely free — no setup fees, no listing fees, no subscription. You only pay a commission on successful orders post-beta.

How do payouts work?

Funds are held in Razorpay Escrow until the order is confirmed shipped. Once dispatched, your earnings are auto-transferred to your connected bank account within 3-5 business days.

Can buyers return products?

Yes. See our Refund & Return Policy for full details. Seller protection is built into the platform.
