💡 What is VyaparSync?

VyaparSync is a revolutionary ONDC-compliant marketplace designed to digitize India's bustling local markets. We connect you directly with authentic local shopkeepers, eliminating middlemen and empowering small businesses.

Our platform brings the vibrant experience of Indian bazaars online, offering real-time inventory and live-streaming commerce straight from the shopkeeper's hands to yours.

🏪 Markets We Support

We are rapidly expanding across major local markets to bring you the best selection of authentic goods. Currently supported markets include:

Chandni Chowk, Delhi Lajpat Nagar, Delhi Sarojini Nagar, Delhi Sadar Bazaar, Delhi Johari Bazaar, Jaipur Commercial Street, Bangalore Crawford Market, Mumbai

🚚 How Delivery Works

We've partnered with reliable ONDC logistics networks like Delhivery, Dunzo, and Shadowfax to ensure your items arrive safely and swiftly.

  • Local Delivery: Same-day or next-day delivery for items ordered from your city's local markets.
  • Pan-India Shipping: 3-5 days delivery for authentic goods ordered from across the country.
  • Live Tracking: Real-time GPS tracking from the seller's shop directly to your doorstep.
