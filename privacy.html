Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and protect your privacy at VyaparSync.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address provided during registration or checkout.
  • Payment Information: We do not store your credit card or UPI details. Payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay.
  • Usage Data: Device information, IP address, browsing activity, and location data (if permitted by you) to enhance the marketplace experience and route orders effectively via ONDC.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for the operation of the VyaparSync platform:

  • To process transactions and manage your orders.
  • To communicate with you regarding order updates, customer support, and promotional offers.
  • To improve our platform, customize the layout for your device, and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • To share necessary fulfillment information (e.g., delivery address and phone number) with sellers and ONDC logistics partners strictly for delivery purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared with:

  • Sellers: To prepare and dispatch your orders.
  • Logistics Partners: To deliver your orders to your address.
  • Payment Processors: To securely authorize and complete payments.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption and secure server hosting, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data stored on our platform. You may update your account details directly or contact our support team for assistance regarding data privacy concerns.

6. Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

VyaparSync acts as a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023. We collect and process your personal data solely for the purpose of facilitating marketplace transactions, with your explicit consent.

Under the Act, you have the right to:

  • Access information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of your personal data.
  • Request the erasure of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any concerns regarding your data privacy, please contact our Grievance Officer at:

Email: grievance@vyaparsync.com
Address: VyaparSync Legal Dept, New Delhi, India
