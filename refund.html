Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: May 2026. We want every purchase to be a great experience.

🛡️ Our Buyer Guarantee

VyaparSync stands behind every purchase. If something goes wrong — wrong item, damaged goods, or a seller who doesn't ship — we've got you covered. Our escrow system holds your payment until your order is confirmed dispatched, protecting you at every step.

⚡ Key Promise: Your money is never released to the seller until you have proof of dispatch. For disputes, we hold funds until resolution.

✅ Valid Reasons for Return

  • Item received is different from what was ordered
  • Item is damaged or defective on arrival
  • Item is missing parts or accessories
  • Wrong size delivered (for clothing)
  • Seller did not ship within the promised timeframe
  • Counterfeit or significantly not as described

🔄 How to Raise a Return Request

1
Go to Orders → Select the order → Click "Return / Issue"
2
Upload a photo of the item received
3

4

You can also email us at support@vyaparsync.com with your order ID and photos.

💸 Refund Timeline

  • UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm): 1-3 business days
  • Credit / Debit Card: 5-7 business days
  • Net Banking: 3-5 business days
  • VyaparSync Wallet Credits: Instant
Note: Refunds during beta will be processed manually by our team. We aim to resolve all disputes within 48 hours of a complaint being raised.

🏪 Seller Responsibilities

Sellers on VyaparSync are required to:

  • Ship orders within 48 hours of confirmation
  • Upload dispatch proof (photo) when marking an order as shipped
  • Respond to customer queries within 24 hours
  • Accept legitimate returns as per this policy

Sellers who repeatedly fail to meet these standards may have their account suspended and funds withheld pending resolution.
